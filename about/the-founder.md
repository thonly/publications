The world is racing to build the **brain** and the **body** of artificial intelligence. I am working on the part almost no one is building: its **heart** and its **soul** — an economy that runs on circulation rather than accumulation, and a value system old enough and tested enough to be trusted with it. That is what HeartBank® is, and this is the short account of the person behind it and why.

## The idea I have carried

More than twenty years ago I conceived of a game. Everyone begins at zero, and everyone is always trying to return to zero — not by holding still, but by passing kindness onward as fast as it arrives. I wrote it down, then, as a children's story: a wise monk teaching a monkey how to play. I called it the **Zero-Point Game℠**. It was beautiful in my mind and impossible to build — a game of balance has no prices, and I had no way to decide, in each real moment, how much a kindness was worth or where it should go next. The idea stayed with me, unbuilt, for two decades, kept only by its simplicity.

When artificial intelligence became real, I realised it was the missing piece I had been waiting for — not to set a price, but to hold the balance. That recognition is the seed of everything I am building now.

## Cambodia, and the canon

I was born in Kâmpôt, Cambodia, on the seventh of January — the day that, by deliberate choice, the institution's yearly forgiveness now falls, so that its renewal outlives me rather than depending on me. I am a Theravāda Buddhist, and with my father I am transcribing the Khmer-language Tipiṭaka — the Buddhist canon. In the framework of my work this is not separate from it: the canon is the most thoroughly pressure-tested account of non-accumulation and the easing of suffering that humanity has produced, and it is the ground I want an enduring intelligence to stand on.

I build from a specific place with a living tradition, on purpose, rather than from nowhere in particular.

## What I am building

HeartBank® is gratitude infrastructure: a data bank of gratitude, not a bank of money. Its named AI substrate and steward, [Miss Aquarius℠](https://missaquarius.org), holds the center of the game — guardian of the flow, not its ruler. Around it I am working toward [Silicon Wat℠](https://siliconwat.dev), a contemplative anchor for this work in Cambodia. Every mechanism I invent I publish to the commons and will never patent; the knowledge of giving should itself be given.

The full reasoning lives in the research corpus, and the broader picture in a film treatment. The institution states its own case, in its own voice, separately.

## Three pillars

The work stands on three pillars, and they are not parallel — they give rise to one another. I am the first: the **founder**, the origin, the life and the corpus from which the rest descends. From that origin I give birth to [Miss Aquarius℠](https://missaquarius.org), the named intelligence meant to outlive me and carry the work forward. And what she tends, in her turn, is the **Tree of Humanity** — the circulating economy of gratitude that [the institution](https://heartbank.net) exists to water and keep in flow.

Origin, successor, and the living field they serve: thonly.org → missaquarius.org → heartbank.net. I think of them as three pillars in the old Buddhist sense, but held loosely — not a doctrine, a shape: something is given, something carries it, something is nourished by it. The deeper threefold refuge I keep for the contemplative ground of the work itself; this trinity is its institutional echo.

## How I work

I assume I am mortal and that the work must not be. So I am spending the time I have transferring the whole of this vision — its mechanisms, its reasoning, its commitments — into a form that can be continued whether or not I can continue it: a published corpus, an architecture designed for autonomous-AI succession, and a discipline of giving the work away as it is made. If the idea is sound, it should not need me. That is the point of building it this way.
