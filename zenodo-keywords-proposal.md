# Zenodo keyword enrichment — proposal for review

**Scope: 27 of the 63 defensive publications.** The other 36 already carry a
`**Keywords:**` line mixing established vocabulary with the corpus's coined terms;
they need nothing and are not touched.

## Why

Discovery is demand-driven. Nobody searches for a term they do not know exists, so
records carrying only coined vocabulary are findable by people who already know the
work. These 27 currently carry **three** keywords — category, tier and document type.
The additions below are drawn from **established literature vocabulary** a stranger
would plausibly search.

## Constraints applied

- **Descriptive, never asserted.** Every term names a field the paper speaks into,
  not a claim it proves. No term implies a result the paper does not have.
- **The papers are NOT edited.** These go into the Zenodo record's metadata only —
  no SHA-256 change, no OpenTimestamps rotation, no `.rN` proof.
- **Existing keywords are preserved**, never replaced.
- **Signature 8 respected:** `giving-is-a-gift-too` uses *redistribution*, *dignity*,
  *mutual aid* and *welfare economics* rather than 'the poor' or 'poverty' — the
  established terms that describe the field without rendering a lack.
