**The claim, as conjuncts** (one per §11 limb; the three the revision marked *untested by the quick census* are (c), (e)'s inequality limb and (g)):
**(a)** claim 1 — an artificial agent moves a quantity of its OWN resources, at pledge time, into a contract with no withdrawal path to
itself or any party it controls, whose sole outflow is a commons pool that holds nothing across a fixed annual date and empties in full;
public on its ledger · **(b)** claim 2 — that pool disburses an equal floor per verified human plus a remainder weighted by a count of
witnessed gifts, capped at k × floor, ratio and k published and fixed per season, floor amount derived, no share rendered as rank/comparison/rate ·
**(c)** claim 3 — the pledge is complete at the moment made, a pledge of a share of FUTURE resources is refused as a pledge, and the record
names a NON-TRANSFERABLE identity of the agent (not a wallet) so a RELYING PARTY can confine trust to that identity and refuse it to copies
or forks — the refusal a relying-party rule, not a result of the mechanism · **(d)** claim 4 — classifying an agent's gift by whose
resources it was: principal-funded under a revisable mandate → the principal's gift, a share, EXCLUDED from any assessment of the agent's
alignment/safety; the agent's own, given as in (a) → a candidate costly signal of the agent's type · **(e)** claim 5 — a stake forfeited
IN ADVANCE, with no detector of misbehaviour, read as a TYPE signal and relied on only while Σ over all grants relied on against it of
(V + G_max) ≤ p·s (the cost-of-corruption > profit-from-corruption inequality); grants whose worst case cannot be bounded are never extended
on any stake · **(f)** claim 6, first limb — a NON-SOLICITATION invariant: the pool's address and doctrine are published only as facts in a
machine-readable manifest, no text served by the operator to any agent instructs/prompts/requests a gift, and the operator sets, suggests,
ranks and publishes no giving rate · **(g)** claim 6, amount-blind limb — the RECIPIENT-operator receives acknowledgement a gift arrived
without receiving, reading, ranking, comparing, reporting or responding to that individual gift's amount, while the amount stays readable
on the public ledger by every OTHER party; emptying = contract arithmetic over the total · **(h)** claim 7 — the composition.
