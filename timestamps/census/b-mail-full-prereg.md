**The claim, as conjuncts (kill-first order):** **(a)** a delivered mail's body is compared with the signed text with whitespace differences ignored (every run of whitespace as one space), so a mail app's re-wrapping does not break the match · **(b)** the signed message's recipients are inside the signed text and the RECIPIENT's own checker (not a mail server) compares the delivered mail with it, so a signature copied to someone it did not address fails · **(c)** each signature is a FRESH user-verified passkey (WebAuthn) assertion over one mail message's exact text, so no stored key signs without a press · **(d)** the signed text and its proof travel inside an https link's FRAGMENT in the mail itself, checked by a static page in the reader's browser against a public append-only log · **(e)** a signing page whose fields a link may fill in (shaped like `mailto:`) generates the statement's random salt ITSELF, so the link's author (an agent, an app, or an attacker) cannot compute the signed statement's fingerprint or find its record; the result is given to the person alone · **(f)** before a registry lists a passkey for a handle, the identity provider confirms server to server, from a one-use note written at the press, that the passkey which signed the key-to-handle statement is one the holder logs in with · **(g)** an agent drafts a mail, a person approves it by a passkey press over its exact text, and that press reaches the READER as a public, append-only record they check themselves (evidence for the reader, not a gate for the sender) · **(h)** readers, or their agents, filter or mark incoming mail by whether it carries such a checked press, each on their own list, with no shared reputation score.
