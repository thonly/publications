- **(j)** a typed name drawn in a one-stroke script face as strokes into the same pad as a hand drawn with a finger or a mouse
- **(g)** a signed picture's own file, as saved, carries the transparency log's signed receipt from the moment of signing (the record, signed by the log's key, before it is included), replaced by an inclusion proof once the log holds it; a verifier reading a receipt answers *not passed* with the receipt beside it
- **(h)** a brand's emblem may stand in a picture's pixels WITHOUT any code (a faint silhouette, or a small sign beside a person's hand like a ™) only when an invisible watermark carrying the address is written into the same file and read back on the device before the signature; the look is refused where it does not hold
- **(i)** a handwritten mark is refused on a picture whose DECLARED source (the IPTC digital source type) is AI-generated, on the device, at the API and again at the signature, the source being chosen actively (nothing pre-filled) before a hand can be added
- **(b′)** a strip below the picture made of the picture's own bottom edge mirrored and melted into soft color, carrying the QR code and its printed address, its words in the ink that wins on the worst pixel under them
- **(c)** a person's own hand, kept as strokes, drawn inside a QR-bearing emblem's free area (a lobe) in place of the issuer's own sign, stated as no legal signature and no claim
- **(d)** decorative modules OUTSIDE a standard QR, in the same drawing (the emblem's other lobe), carrying an error-corrected coarse fingerprint of the host picture (cells brighter or darker than the median) with the mark's own footprint masked, read offline, so a mark copied onto another picture is told apart
- **(e)** the corner in which a QR's middle finder pattern stands (its quarter-turn), set by the encoder from the address it holds, as a declared class of destination crossed with who drew it, any address outside the issuer's domains refused by the drawing itself
- **(f′)** all of it in one mark, each part the signer's choice, off by default, the whole an address and never a claim
