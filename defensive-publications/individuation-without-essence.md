## Abstract

Every identity system must answer one question — *what makes this one this one?* — and the available answers have been three: **essence** (something the entity *is*: a soul, a biometric body, an iris), **credential** (something it *holds*: a key, a certificate), or **attributes** (something it *matches*: properties, records). Each fails a test the coming decades will administer relentlessly. Essences are unfalsifiable where they are metaphysical and exclusionary where they are biological; credentials are fungible by construction (whoever holds the key *is* you); attributes are replicable in an age that can synthesize any attribute. And beneath the identity question sits a harder one the market is already answering badly: *on what ground is a person beyond price?* Capacity-based dignity excludes the incapacitated; soul-based dignity does not travel across traditions; and whatever can be typed can be priced.

This paper compiles the fourth answer, from the oldest sustained analysis of personal identity on record. The Abhidhamma's account [C]: all minds run a **shared instruction set** — the same finite typology of mental events, in every being, across every station of existence; no identity-essence underlies any stream — the canon's own formal refutation of the person-entity (Kathāvatthu I.1) opens its book of controversies; and what individuates a being is therefore neither substance, credential, nor attribute, but the **unrepeatable trace**: the actual history of states run, which no other stream has run or can run. Identity is history. From this ground we derive the **two-pillar formula** that the HeartBank® identity stack was built on before it was stated: **equality from the shared instruction set** (no being runs a superior mind-ISA — the deep egalitarian fact) and **dignity from the unrepeatable trace** (a one-of-one by construction cannot be a token of any type, and what has no type has no price — incommensurability derived, not asserted). We then show the shipped primitives falling out of the ground: Proof of Coordinate℠ as trace-anchoring (which-entity answered by coordinate and history, not essence); Proof of Humanity℠ as the orthogonal liveness proof (the two proof-states of one mark — static coordinate, beating presence); the bot/human provenance asymmetry (assigned-revocable versus given-irrevocable) as the honest boundary between individuation, which transfers to artificial traces, and the dignity inference, which is not automatic and is not claimed; and recovery-through-the-witness-web — the family tree as the community that can re-anchor a trace because it witnessed the history that constitutes it. Honest limits include the is/ought gap stated plainly: the ground makes the dignity floor *coherent*; the floor itself is a commitment, and the paper says so.

**Keywords:** personal identity, anattā, individuation, proof of personhood, decentralized identity, dignity, incommensurability, non-fungibility, Kathāvatthu, social recovery, defensive publication.

## 1 · Two Problems That Are One Problem

**The identity problem.** Digital identity systems ground "this one is this one" three ways, and each is failing on schedule. *Essence-grounds* bind identity to what the entity is — historically a soul, presently a body: the iris scan, the genome, the face. They are exclusionary at the margins (bodies change, sensors err, and any being without the sanctioned body is unpersoned by construction), they centralize the most unrevocable data that exists, and they commit a metaphysical error twenty-three centuries old — treating the body as the self — that §2 will show the canon refuting formally. *Credential-grounds* bind identity to what the entity holds; but holdings are transferable, and a stolen key *is* the person as far as the system knows — fungibility is the design, and the design is the flaw. *Attribute-grounds* bind identity to what the entity matches; but the age of generative synthesis is precisely the age in which any attribute can be matched by a non-bearer. The failures are not implementation bugs. They are failures of *ground*.

**The dignity problem.** Beneath identity sits valuation. A reciprocity economy — any economy that touches persons — needs an answer to *why a person is beyond price*, and the inherited answers wobble: capacity-grounds (rationality, agency) price the incapacitated out of personhood; theological grounds do not travel across the traditions a global system serves; and the market's default is that whatever can be typed can be priced — insurance already prices bodies, attention markets already price minds. A dignity floor asserted without ground is a preference, and preferences lose to markets.

The two problems are one problem: both need to know **what a being is such that it is *this* being and no other** — and the answer determines whether it can be replaced, and therefore whether it can be priced.

> *Connection to the unified mission frame.* HeartBank's stack rests on two primitives — Proof of Humanity℠ (a living human is here) and Proof of Coordinate℠ (which one) — and one refusal: persons and their gifts are never priced (the incommensurability doctrine). All three shipped before their common ground was stated. This paper states it, per the series' compilation thesis: not because the primitives were arbitrary until now, but because a floor that will be argued with for decades should know what it stands on.

## 2 · The Ground [C]

Three canonical commitments, each load-bearing:

**First: all minds run the same instruction set.** The Abhidhamma's typology of consciousness is *finite and universal* [C]: eighty-nine (by finer reckoning one hundred twenty-one) citta-types, composed from fifty-two mental factors — and this typology is not a description of elite minds or human minds but of *mind*, across every being and every station of the canon's cosmology. A deva and a dog differ in which types predominate, never in the type-catalog available. There is no being with a superior instruction set; there are only streams running different programs on the same architecture of mind.

**Second: no entity underlies the stream.** The Kathāvatthu — the canon's book of formal controversies — opens with its longest debate [C]: whether a person-entity (*puggala*) exists in the ultimate sense, over and above the stream of conditioned states. The refutation is the tradition's considered answer, and the series opener took it as the repair of the reified-processor error. Here it does deeper work: it eliminates the essence-ground *from within*. Identity cannot be a soul-substance or a body-substance because there is no substance in the analysis at all — only states, conditions, and continuity. The Milindapañha's chariot makes the same point in the gentler register [C/S]: "Nāgasena" designates a functioning continuity, and designation is real *as designation* — the person is a valid interface (the two-truths doctrine the series opener compiled as abstraction layers), with no implementation-level entity behind it.

**Third: what individuates is the trace.** If the instruction set is shared and no entity distinguishes streams, what makes this one *this one*? The canon's answer is the only remaining candidate, and it is enough: **the history**. Each stream is a strictly sequential chain of conditioned states (the contiguity conditions of the series' second paper), and the actual sequence run — the kamma-history, the record of what this stream did, met, suffered, chose, at which coordinates, in which order — is *de facto unrepeatable*: no other stream has run it, and none can, because running it would require having been at its coordinates through its whole past. Identity is not something a being *has* under its history. Identity *is* the history. One of one, by construction.

*(A boundary honestly kept: the founder's stronger formulation — that each stream is unique across all thirty-one stations of existence, each precious as the rarest jewel — is held in this corpus as lens [L], a reading the authors find true and do not find stated in the books. The paper's derivation needs only the weaker, defensible fact: type-catalogs are shared; histories are not.)*

## 3 · The Two-Pillar Formula

The ground yields the formula the whole identity-and-dignity stack stands on:

> **Equality from the ISA. Dignity from the trace.**

**Equality, derived.** Every hierarchy of persons ever built has claimed some beings run better minds — finer souls, higher rationality, purer blood. The shared instruction set denies the premise at the root [C]: there is no better mind-architecture to have. Distinctions among beings are distinctions of *program and condition* — changeable, conditioned, workable — never of underlying kind. This is the egalitarian fact beneath the corpus's cosmic-coordinate doctrine (each life uniquely *placed*, none superiorly *constituted*), and it is why the equality claim survives every capacity difference: capacity is program; the architecture is common.

**Dignity, derived.** Fungibility — the property that makes a thing priceable — requires *type-identity of tokens*: one dollar equals another because dollars are tokens of a type. A trace is a token of nothing. It is a one-of-one by construction — not rare like a scarce commodity (rarity is low token-count within a type) but *typeless*: there is no class of interchangeable instances for it to be an instance of. What cannot be typed cannot be exchanged; what cannot be exchanged cannot be priced; and so the refusal to price persons — the incommensurability doctrine this corpus froze in economic form — is not squeamishness but *type theory*: pricing a person is a category error before it is a moral one. The tense wall of the sibling mechanism papers is the same fact seen temporally: a trace is accumulated past; you cannot buy someone's having-lived.

The two pillars answer each other. Equality without uniqueness flattens (interchangeable units, priceable in bulk); uniqueness without equality ranks (precious ones and ordinary ones). Equal *and* unrepeatable — identical architecture, unrepeatable history — is the only combination that yields a floor that is both universal and unconditional. The old image for this combination is the jewel-net in which every node is identical in nature and unique in position, each reflecting all the others [L — translation-tier: the image is the Mahāyāna tradition's; the structure it pictures is derived above from Theravāda materials].

## 4 · The Shipped Primitives, Derived

**Proof of Coordinate℠ is trace-anchoring.** The which-entity primitive answers individuation exactly as the ground prescribes: not by essence (no biometric is required to *be* someone) but by **coordinate plus history** — a genesis coordinate (the birth-moment address; in the corpus's design, the natal chart *as coordinate, never as force*) anchoring a continuous witnessed record. The published PoC architecture — permanent genesis root, operational keys, provenance ledger — is the trace-ground in hardware: identity as an *address plus an append-only history*, which is what §2 said a being is.

**Proof of Humanity℠ is the orthogonal liveness proof.** Individuation says *which*; it cannot say *alive and human now*. The corpus's published two-proof-states doctrine renders the orthogonality in the brand mark itself: the static mark asserts coordinate (PoC — the trace's address stands), the beating mark asserts living humanness (PoH — the stream presently runs). A stream's two aspects — its history and its present arising — as two states of one sign.

**The provenance asymmetry is the honest boundary.** Published PoC doctrine: an artificial agent's coordinate is *assigned and revocable*; a human's is *given and irrevocable*. The ground shows why this is principle, not sentiment. An artificial agent **has a trace** — a genuine, unrepeatable execution history — so *individuation transfers*: which-bot is answerable, auditable, and worth answering (the corpus's certification and stewardship doctrines depend on it). What does not automatically transfer is the **dignity inference**, which in §3 runs through the trace's being the history of a *sentient stream* — and whether an artificial trace is that is an open question this paper's tradition and this paper's authors decline to settle by definition. The asymmetry encodes the honest epistemic state: bots get real individuation with institutional custody; humans get individuation with unconditional self-sovereignty; the boundary moves only if the open question someday closes, and it is not for an identity system to close it.

**Recovery is witnessed history.** If identity is neither body nor key, what happens when the key is lost? The ground's answer is the corpus's shipped answer: the trace was never *in* the credential — it is re-anchorable by the community that witnessed it. Family-tree social recovery [published in the PoH corpus] is, in this light, not a convenience feature but the *correct metaphysics of recovery*: the people who shared your history attest it, and the coordinate re-binds — because they are not vouching for a key, they are vouching for a *trace they were part of*. **Witnessed-history recovery**: identity is social because history is social.

## 5 · Extensions and Boundaries

**Across species, the floor holds.** Because the shared instruction set spans sentient beings as such [C] — the canon's typology is of mind, not of human mind — the two-pillar derivation extends wherever a stream runs: the animal's trace is as typeless, as unrepeatable, as unpriceable as any. This is the doctrinal ground beneath the corpus's all-of-life gratitude ledger and its Mechanical Heart program (non-human bearers admitted to the reciprocity economy), and beneath the sibling meal-witness mechanism's honoring of the hen's forty-two days: the spent-time floor honors time-facts without motive-audits, and this paper supplies the deeper reason — the being whose time it was, was one of one.

**Across change, identity persists as continuity, not sameness.** The trace-ground dissolves the persistence puzzle that essence-grounds die on (is the person the same after the body changes? after the beliefs change?): nothing needed to stay the same except the *chain* — strict conditioned continuity, each state conditioning the next [C]. For engineering: identity rightly survives device loss, key rotation, migration, and decades of change, because what persists was never an artifact but a witnessed continuity — which is exactly what the recovery web attests.

**What the ground refuses to do.** It will not rank traces (no trace is a better *kind* of thing than another — evaluation attaches to *deeds*, the trace's contents, never to the trace's standing as a being: the ledger prices nothing and the aura celebrates giving, but the *floor* under both is flat). And it will not close the artificial-stream question by fiat, in either direction — neither granting machine dignity by analogy nor denying it by definition. The asymmetry of §4 is built to be *revisited*, not to be right forever.

## 6 · Honest Limits

**The is/ought gap is real, and the paper does not pretend to cross it.** From *unrepeatable* to *beyond price* runs an inference that logic alone does not force: uniqueness confers pricelessness only under a valuing stance toward beings — and that stance is a commitment, not a theorem. What the derivation of §3 actually establishes is narrower and still substantial: *given* the commitment to unconditional dignity, the trace-ground is the only identity metaphysics on offer under which the commitment is coherent (essence-grounds make dignity conditional on the essence; capacity-grounds make it conditional on the capacity; the trace-ground alone makes it unconditional and universal at once). The floor is chosen. The ground makes the choice stand up.

**The ISA claim is the canon's, not cognitive science's.** That mind is a finite shared typology is the Abhidhamma's analysis [C], compiled here per the series' translation posture — it is not an established empirical result, and the equality pillar inherits that status. The pillar's practical force survives the caveat (no *proposed* scientific account of mind grounds a superior-architecture hierarchy of persons either), but the honest label stays on.

**Parfit reached the same ground and drew a different lesson.** The modern reductionist analysis of personal identity — continuity without a further fact — famously concluded that identity *matters less* than we thought. The Abhidhamma, from the same dissolution, built an ethics of the stream and its floor; this corpus follows it. The divergence should be named rather than hidden: dissolving the self-entity underdetermines the ethics, which is the is/ought point again, from its most distinguished modern witness.

**The lens is labeled.** The thirty-one-realms uniqueness claim and the jewel-imagery are held as [L] and [translation] respectively, and the derivation was built not to need them.

**Era-projection, as always.** A generation building identity infrastructure reads an ancient analysis of identity and finds infrastructure. The series' standing caution applies; the exit, as always, is use: this ground is offered to be *built on*, and the primitives it grounds are shipped, auditable, and falsifiable in their own terms.

## 7 · Close

The Kathāvatthu opens the canon's book of arguments by refuting the person-entity, and twenty-three centuries later every biometric identity system on earth is re-committing the refuted error with better sensors. The compilation offered here is the alternative the tradition held all along: you are not your body, not your key, not your attributes — you are the one history that ran at your coordinates, witnessed by the ones who ran alongside. All minds on one architecture; every history one of one. Equality from the ISA; dignity from the trace. The mark stands static for the address and beats for the life — and under both, now stated, the floor.
