## Abstract

Every existing system that connects consumption to conscience runs on the same mechanism: **quantify the harm, price the remedy, sell the discharge.** Carbon offsets convert emissions into purchasable absolution; their documented failure mode is *moral licensing* — the offset does not merely fail to reduce consumption; it licenses more of it. The same architecture has been tried before, at civilizational scale, under an older name: the indulgence. The failure is not in the accounting's accuracy. It is in the *existence* of the account — the moment a remedy is denominated in the same ledger as the harm, a conversion rate forms, and conscience becomes a market.

This paper specifies the inverse mechanism, designed for the most universal consumption event there is: the meal. A photograph of food is resolved into its ingredient beings — the plants and animals whose lifespans became the eater's nourishment — and each being's **time-sacrifice** is presented as a factual *biography* (a broiler hen: forty-two days; her species: up to eight years), individually, without aggregation into any balance. The eater is invited to answer the witnessed sacrifice with a **gratitude practice in nature** — a walking or sitting meditation, dedicated to the beings consumed — whose recommended form is a **ceremonial unit** set by a neutral automated agent and *never scaled to the meal*. Nothing is discharged, because the mechanism maintains **no account to discharge**: the sacrifice is witnessed, the acknowledgment is made, and the two are never entered into a common ledger. We name this architecture **sacrifice-witness without discharge**, and we argue it is the only shape under which a consumption-conscience system avoids becoming an indulgence market: the anti-offset.

The paper specifies the full mechanism — the witness engine and its two-source data architecture (a curated food-species table for the eaten; wild-organism identification for the living encountered at the practice site); the non-equation and its no-balance invariant; the two-movement dedication grammar (to the departed beings consumed, and outward to a community that marks its rejoicing with location pins rather than scores); the phoneless bracketing that keeps the application structurally absent during the practice it frames; and the child-safe mode. Eight claims are enumerated as prior art and dedicated to the public domain; three predictions are pre-registered, including the falsifiable wager that distinguishes this design from every offset system: **witnessed sacrifice without discharge will not license increased consumption.** The authors will not seek patent.

**Keywords:** gratitude practice, mindful eating, moral licensing, carbon offset, indulgence, time-sacrifice, merit dedication, meal-anchored habit, species identification, contemplative technology, defensive publication.

## 1 · The Problem: The Householder's Gap and the Offset Trap

Two failures motivate this mechanism, one contemplative and one economic.

**The householder's gap.** The retreat works because it removes the choice: meals are served, sits are scheduled, and the practitioner's responsibilities wait outside the gate. The householder's life restores the choice and the practice loses its anchor — not for lack of sincerity but for lack of a *daily trigger* that survives contact with obligation. Meditation applications attack this problem with streaks, reminders, and gamified continuity, importing the attention economy's retention machinery into the one domain that exists to dissolve it. The result is well documented in the category's retention curves: the streak works until it breaks, and when it breaks it takes the practice with it, because the practice was anchored to the *score* rather than to anything in the practitioner's actual day.

There is, however, a trigger that requires no streak, no reminder, and no gamification, because it is older than fire and arrives two to three times daily without fail: **the meal.** Nearly every contemplative tradition discovered this anchor centuries ago — the meal-reflection is among the most widespread of all lay practices (§13) — and modern habit research rediscovered it as the reliability of stacking a new behavior on an invariant daily event. The mechanism specified here is, at bottom, the oldest anchor connected to the oldest gratitude.

**The offset trap.** The economic failure is subtler and more dangerous. Systems that connect consumption to conscience almost universally adopt the *offset architecture*: measure the harm in some unit, price a remedy in the same unit, and let the consumer purchase equivalence. Carbon offsets are the contemporary flagship, and the empirical literature on *moral licensing* records the architecture's characteristic failure: establishing moral credit in one column licenses moral relaxation in another. Consumers who purchase green products cheat and steal more in subsequent tasks than those who merely viewed them (Mazar & Zhong 2010); moral self-licensing is a robust, replicated effect across domains (Merritt, Effron & Monin 2010). The offset does not merely leak; it *inverts* — the discharge mechanism becomes a consumption license. The historical anti-example is exact enough that we state it plainly: an architecture that quantifies transgression and sells its remission is an **indulgence market**, and the Reformation is the case study in how such markets end.

The trap generalizes to any contemplative-consumption design. A meal application that computes the "sacrifice cost" of eating and lets meditation "burn it off" — the naive gym metaphor — has built an indulgence market for the dinner table: *meditate it off, eat what you want.* Its own stated purpose (mindful, bounded eating) would be structurally defeated by its own mechanism. The design problem, precisely stated: **how does a system make the sacrifice embodied in consumption fully visible, and invite a genuine response to it, without ever forming the exchange rate that converts response into license?**

The answer specified below inherits its spine from doctrine this corpus has already published: the two currencies of the reciprocity economy are *coupled but never convertible*, and the coupling can only be held open by a neutral agent whose recommendations are blind to quantity (the incommensurability-preserving coupler). This paper is that coupler's third instance. The first held money and time apart while letting gratitude flow between them; the second held one person's hour apart from another's; this one holds the largest pair of all: **what nature gives, and what a human can answer.**

> *Connection to the unified mission frame.* HeartBank's mission is the restoration of humanity to the middle way at population scale. The meal is where every human meets the natural world's sacrifice three times a day, almost always without seeing it; the practice-gap is where modern householders lose contact with the contemplative traditions that once structured that seeing. One mechanism that closes both — visibility without guilt-accounting, practice without gamification — is mission-native infrastructure, and it is deliberately the family's first public surface: the world's introduction to a gratitude economy should be a practice that asks for nothing and settles nothing.

## 2 · The Mechanism: One Loop, Two Movements

### 2.1 · The canonical scene

A father photographs lunch at the kitchen table. The application names the beings on the plate: *rice — one hundred twenty days, its whole season; a hen — forty-two days.* He taps once more and reads that her kind can live eight years. No number turns red; nothing is totaled; nothing is owed. That evening he walks the neighborhood with his daughter — the application suggested a short walk, as it would have after any meal — and before they leave, she pins the park at the corner, coarse, the whole block. Phones go into pockets. They walk, and they dedicate the walk to the hen and the rice, by name. At the park the daughter points the camera at a tree afterward and learns it is a tamarind, and that the sparrows in it are the hen's distant kin; she dedicates a wish to the sparrows too. At home, the rabbit with the jade bowl asks her what she saw, and never asks how long they walked. The father shares the dedication; by morning, an aunt in Kâmpôt has answered it with a single pin on the map — *I rejoiced with this* — and nothing else. No score changed anywhere in this scene, because no score exists.

## 3 · The Witness Engine

### 3.1 · From photograph to beings

The user photographs the meal. A food-recognition model — the calorie-application class of vision model, not a wild-organism identifier — resolves the photograph into an ingredient list, and the ingredients are mapped to their source species. The output of this stage is not a nutritional panel but a **cast of beings**: the rice plant, the hen, the carrot, the fish.

### 3.2 · The biography, and the two numbers

For each being, the witness presents a short biography whose centerpiece is the being's **time-sacrifice**: the span of life that became this food. The load-bearing data decision is that this is the *farmed* time — the life actually lived — not the species' natural potential:

- a broiler hen: **~42 days** (her species: up to ~8 years)
- a market pig: **~6 months** (his species: 15–20 years)
- a carrot: **~70 days** — its whole intended span
- rice: **~120 days** — likewise complete

The farmed number is the honest primary fact: it is the time that was actually given. The natural span is available one layer deeper, for the eater who wants the fuller biography — and the juxtaposition, where it exists, is presented *bare*. The mechanism's discipline here is **visibility, not verdict**: the numbers are facts; the application attaches no judgment, no red badge, no moral color-coding. (The design constraint this discipline serves — and the doctrinal honesty behind it — is stated in §12.) Wild-caught beings carry their conservation status as an additional bare fact. Where the recognition model is uncertain, the biography says so: a witness that guesses confidently is not a witness.

### 3.3 · What is deliberately absent: the total

The witness presents beings *severally*. There is no meal total, no daily total, no lifetime total, no "sacrifice score." This is not an interface preference; it is the mechanism's central invariant (§4.2), enforced at the data layer: **the schema contains no accumulator.** The most important engineering decision in this paper is a field that does not exist.

### 3.4 · The two-source data architecture

The engine draws on two deliberately separate sources, mirroring the loop's two columns:

**The plate — a curated food-species table.** Humanity's diet concentrates on a remarkably small set of species; on the order of one to two hundred cover nearly every meal served on earth. Lifespan and husbandry data for these species is well-established but scattered (animal longevity in the AnAge database; trait aggregation in the Encyclopedia of Life's TraitBank; aquatic species in FishBase; farmed durations in the agronomy literature) and is therefore *curated once* into a maintained table — with per-entry sourcing, regional variance noted, and estimates marked as estimates — rather than fetched live from any single API that does not in fact hold it.

**The park — wild-organism identification.** At the practice site, a separate identification layer — the iNaturalist class of computer vision, built precisely for living organisms photographed in nature — lets the practitioner meet the beings *around* them: the trees, birds, and plants of the place where the dedication is made. The two sources never mix roles: the table knows the departed; the identifier knows the living. §7 develops what the park layer is for.

## 4 · The Non-Equation

### 4.1 · The ceremonial acknowledgment unit

Having witnessed the meal's beings, the user is invited to answer with a gratitude practice in nature — a walking meditation through the neighborhood, a sitting meditation in a park. The invitation carries a recommended form — *a short walk; a sit of modest length* — issued by the system's neutral agent (Miss Aquarius℠, the same agent whose sole-set neutral units anchor the corpus's other coupling mechanisms). The recommendation is **ceremonial**: a flat, standard practice-form, the same after a snack as after a feast, **never computed from the meal's contents, count, or biography.**

This is the non-equation, and its rationale is doctrinal before it is defensive. Under the corpus's published tense doctrine, what the eater received is *stored* lifespan — the completed past of beings now departed — and the dead cannot be repaid: no quantity of present practice converts into restored past life. A recommendation scaled to the meal would assert precisely the conversion that is impossible, and every downstream pathology of §1's offset trap would follow from that single assertion. The ceremonial unit refuses the conversion at the root: the practice answers the gift; it does not *price* it. In the corpus's inherited language: the hour of presence is *a new gift in the beings' memory* — never the old gift refunded.

### 4.2 · The no-balance invariant

The non-equation is enforced by an invariant stated here as prior art in its strongest form:

> **The system maintains no account in which consumed sacrifice and performed practice are commensurable.** There is no balance that meals increment and meditations decrement; no "remaining," "owed," "neutral," or "net" state; no per-meal or per-period total of either column. What the system keeps is an append-only *witness log* — meals witnessed, practices made, dedications spoken — a record of moments, not a ledger of amounts.

Three interface corollaries, each individually claimed:

1. **No completion arithmetic.** The state after a practice is "witnessed and answered," never "cleared" or "offset." Language of settlement is excluded from every surface.
2. **No duration display.** The practice session is neither timed on-screen, displayed in minutes, nor ranked; the log records *that* a practice was made and *to whom* it was dedicated — the moment, not the meter.
3. **No streaks.** Continuity mechanics are excluded by design. The meal itself is the recurrence engine (§1); a streak would re-anchor the practice to a score and import the exact breakage dynamics the meal-anchor exists to escape. The only calendar the mechanism observes is a periodic *return to zero* — an emptying, not an accumulation.

The full **refusal set**, enumerated as design commitments (the mechanism is defined as much by what it will not build as by what it builds): no balance · no discharge rate · no completion arithmetic · no duration display · no streaks · no leaderboards or rankings · no per-user totals · no dietary verdicts or prescriptions · no comment threads on dedications (the presence-mark is the *only* response primitive — removing, by construction, the surface on which food-shaming and moral brigading would otherwise occur) · no advertising on any witness or practice surface. Each refusal is individually load-bearing; together they are the non-equation's enforcement.

### 4.3 · Why this is the anti-offset

With the non-equation and the no-balance invariant in place, the licensing pathway is structurally severed. Moral licensing requires a *credit* — a represented quantity of moral standing that consumption can spend. This mechanism represents no credit: the practice produces no fungible token, no score, no offset certificate, nothing that could be mentally banked against the next indulgence. What it produces is a *memory of having seen* — and the pre-registered wager of this design (P-G1, §10) is that seeing without settling shifts consumption *down or not at all*, where settling reliably shifts it up.

The economic statement of the same point, in the corpus's terms: the offset architecture is *coupling with convertibility* — the exact configuration this corpus's coupler doctrine identifies as the kill vector in every domain it touches. The anti-offset is coupling *without* convertibility: the meal and the practice are joined by a neutral agent that transmits gratitude between them while refusing, by construction, to transmit price.

## 5 · The Dedication Grammar

### 5.1 · The vertical movement: dedication to the departed

Before the practice, the user pins a location (coarse by default). During the practice, the user dedicates the walk or sit **to the beings witnessed at the meal** — beings that are, without exception, departed. The mechanism's cultural depth here is not incidental and §13 states its lineage precisely; the design fact is that the dedication is the practice's *aim*, transforming a generic mindfulness session into a directed act of gratitude with named recipients.

### 5.2 · The horizontal movement: the share and the presence-marks

After the practice, the user may broadcast the dedication — the place (coarse), the beings, optionally the reflection that accompanied it. Others who encounter the broadcast may answer with a **presence-mark**: a location pin that says *I rejoiced with this*, and nothing else. Presence-marks carry no count toward any score, confer no rank, and accumulate no leaderboard; a map of them is a map of where rejoicing happened, legible as geography and illegible as competition. The pin, like the practice, marks a moment.

Two guards inherited from the corpus's witness-layer doctrine travel with the broadcast: the share is **opt-in and secondary** (the private practice is complete without it), and provenance is human-verified (a broadcast dedication is signed by a verified human; the witness layer of a gratitude economy cannot be synthetic).

### 5.3 · What the horizontal movement is not

The broadcast layer is explicitly not a feed optimized for watch-time, not a follower-count surface, and not a virality engine; its success metric is *practices inspired*, the only downstream event worth counting. The design anticipates the performativity critique and answers it structurally: with no scores, no streaks, no duration display, and no per-user totals, the surface offers performance almost nothing to perform *for*.

## 6 · Phoneless Bracketing

The deepest contradiction available to a meditation application is to place a screen at the center of the one activity that exists to put the screen down. The mechanism resolves this by **bracketing**: the application acts *before* the practice (the witness, the invitation, the pin) and *after* it (the log, the optional share), and is **structurally absent during it**. The canonical session is: *pin → pocket → practice → return.* No in-practice screen, no guided-audio dependency in the core loop, no live timer, no mid-session interaction of any kind. The application is the frame, never the mediator; the practice happens between a person and a place.

This is claimed as an architectural commitment, not a usage suggestion: the core loop is designed so that no feature *requires* the device during practice, and session integrity never depends on in-practice device state. (A consequence with independent value: the practice is fully functional where devices fail — the signal-poor park, the airplane-mode retreat, the pocket that stays closed.)

## 7 · The Park Layer, the Living Kin, and the Child

### 7.1 · The living kin

The witness introduced the eater to beings who are gone. The park layer introduces them to the same families, alive: the identification camera, used before or after the phoneless practice — never during — resolves the trees, birds, and plants of the practice site. The design sentence the feature exists to deliver: **the being on your plate has living kin in this park.** The dedication made to the departed is thereby surrounded by the living, and the practitioner's gratitude acquires a second, present-tense address: dedication may be extended to the living beings encountered at the site — met, named, and included.

### 7.2 · Citizen science as giving-forward

Identifications made at practice sites are, at the user's choice, contributable to public biodiversity databases — the observation networks on which the identification models themselves are trained. The gratitude practice thereby feeds the commons that enables it: **citizen science as the give-forward**, the corpus's circulation atom appearing unforced at the data layer. This is a v2 surface and is claimed as architecture, not promised as launch scope.

### 7.3 · The child's mode

The practice is teachable to children, and the mechanism's teacher is already constituted: the HeartBank family's mascot — a rabbit holding a bowl, saying grace — whose standing constitutional boundary in this corpus is exact for the role: *he demonstrates and teaches; he never keeps score.* The character who says grace but never meters the sacrifice is the non-equation in a form a five-year-old can love.

The child's mode is governed by a hard privacy floor, stated as a claim because it is a design commitment and not merely compliance: **family-scoped only — no public pins, no location broadcast, no public sharing of any kind from a child's account.** A child dedicates, learns, and rejoices inside the family circle; the horizontal movement's public surface is an adult surface. Identification features for children follow the established child-safe pattern of the species-identification category (accountless, on-device where feasible).

### 7.4 · The privacy floor generally

Meal photographs are processed on-device where the platform permits, and are never required to leave the device; what leaves is what the user chooses to dedicate and share. Location pins are coarse by default, delayable, and anonymizable. The witness is private by construction; only the dedication is ever public, and only by choice.

### 7.5 · The session light (design layer; no claim added)

*Added 2026-07-23. This subsection describes a design layer above the mechanism specified in §§2–7. It adds no claim to §8 and amends none; every invariant of §4 governs it unchanged.*

The park layer poses a design problem the witness layer does not. The witness is complete in a moment — a meal is seen, its beings are named, the practitioner sits. The walk is extended, voluntary, and easy to abandon; it is precisely the surface on which a gratitude application is most tempted to reach for the retention mechanics the rest of this paper refuses.

The resolution keeps the refusal and supplies the pull from the opposite direction. The walk carries a **session state that begins full and descends toward empty as the practitioner gives** — each living kin met and dedicated to, each wish sent, each site identification contributed to the commons draws it down. The state is **never rendered as a number**. It is rendered as **colour**: a light that travels the palette as the session empties, arriving at white when nothing is left held. A practitioner reads their session the way one reads a sky, not the way one reads a balance.

Six commitments hold this consistent with the mechanism rather than making it an exception:

**The firewall (load-bearing).** The session light exists **only on the horizontal movement** — the walk toward the living kin (§5, §7.1). It never appears on the vertical movement, and no session state is ever computed over a meal. Nothing about the consumed beings is emptied, settled, or whitened; §4's non-equation is untouched because the light never touches the meal. What descends is the practitioner's own holding of goodwill toward the living, never a debt to the departed.

**Descent, not accumulation.** The state's motion is downward and its terminus is zero. It is not a score that grows with practice; it is held goodwill that the practice *spends*. A practitioner who gives nothing keeps a full light and has thereby accumulated nothing; a practitioner who gives everything ends at white and carries nothing forward. Whatever goes unspent returns with the next session's beginning rather than banking — §4's return-to-zero calendar in miniature, per session rather than per year.

**Colour is not a metric.** The light carries no numeral, no total, no rank, no comparison, and no continuity mechanic. It is not summed across sessions, not displayed as a history, and not comparable between practitioners: two people at white have each finished their own session and stand in no relation whatever. This is §5's presence-mark discipline applied to the session's own state — a mark that something happened, carrying no measure of how much.

**Two lights, one palette (a naming discipline).** This corpus already specifies a persistent visual signal earned by a person's real giving history across the wider system. The session light is a **different signal drawn in the same palette**: persistent versus ephemeral, history versus session, earned versus spent. The two must remain visually distinct in form, and product copy must never call the session light by the persistent signal's name. Collapsing them would silently convert an unscored session state into a reputation — the exact failure this subsection exists to prevent.

**The vow, and the silence it does not inspect.** A practitioner may close a session by committing to sit. The **commitment**, not the sit, is what the design registers: the light resolves to its terminal state at the moment of the resolve, and the sit itself passes unobserved, unverified, and untimed, exactly as §6 requires. This is deliberate rather than approximate. A resolve is something a person can honestly declare; the quality of a silence is not something an application can know, and a design that tried to know it would have to put the screen back inside the practice. A practitioner who declares the vow and does not sit has gained a colour and nothing else — there is no economic surface to defraud, which is why an honest declaration is sturdy enough to build on.

**No walk is required.** A session may consist of the vow alone. Rain, illness, limited mobility, grief, and a biodiversity-poor neighbourhood are each sufficient reason, and none of them caps the light: the terminal state is reachable by resolve from anywhere. The walk's pull is the delight of meeting the living kin, never privileged access to the colour. This is stated as a commitment because the alternative quietly rations the practice by geography, and the practitioners with the fewest parks are seldom the ones with the most of anything else.

## 8 · The Claims

Enumerated as prior art; each is claimed severally and in combination:

1. **The sacrifice-witness engine:** photographic meal recognition resolved to source species, each presented as a per-being lifespan biography — farmed time-to-harvest as the primary fact, natural species lifespan as a distinct secondary fact, conservation status for wild-caught beings, and explicit uncertainty language under low recognition confidence — with beings presented severally and **no aggregate quantity computed or displayed**.
2. **The ceremonial acknowledgment unit:** a contemplative-practice recommendation issued by a neutral automated agent as a flat, standard form that is **deliberately blind to the consumed sacrifice's magnitude, count, and duration** — never scaled to the meal.
3. **The no-balance invariant:** an architecture whose data schema contains no accumulator commensurating consumption and practice — no balance, no net state, no settlement language, no duration display, no streak or continuity mechanic — maintaining instead an append-only witness log of moments, with a periodic return-to-zero calendar as the only temporal structure.
4. **The two-movement dedication grammar:** a location-pinned contemplative session dedicated to the specific beings witnessed at a meal (the vertical movement, to the departed), optionally broadcast as a merit-share to which others respond with **presence-marks** — location pins carrying no score, rank, or count (the horizontal movement, to the living).
5. **Phoneless bracketing:** a session architecture in which the mediating application operates only before and after the practice, with no in-practice screen dependency, no live timer, and no mid-session interaction required for session integrity.
6. **The two-source data architecture:** a curated, sourced food-species table (farmed durations + natural lifespans + biographies) serving the consumption-witness function, held separate from a wild-organism identification layer serving the practice-site function, with the living-kin linkage between them ("the being on your plate has living kin in this park") and optional contribution of site identifications to public biodiversity databases.
7. **The child-safe dedication mode:** a family-scoped child mode with no public pins, no location broadcast, and no public sharing, taught by a mascot character constitutionally barred from scorekeeping.
8. **The anti-offset composition:** the combination of claims 1–5 as a consumption-conscience system that makes harm/gift fully visible while structurally excluding the formation of any discharge rate — the coupling-without-convertibility architecture applied to the consumption–practice pair.

## 9 · Prior Art, Generously

**The offset architecture (the anti-example).** Carbon-offset consumer products and markets; their moral-licensing literature (Mazar & Zhong 2010; Merritt, Effron & Monin 2010) and integrity critiques; and the historical indulgence as the architecture's oldest instance. This paper's mechanism is defined *against* this class, and the class is therefore its most important prior art.

**Food-recognition applications.** The calorie-scanning class (MyFitnessPal and successors; the photograph-to-nutrition wave) established the interaction primitive this mechanism inherits — photograph the plate, receive an analysis — and equally established the pathology this mechanism must not inherit: quantified tracking's slide into obsession and moralized eating (§12).

**Species identification.** iNaturalist and its computer-vision identification; Seek's accountless, child-safe on-device pattern; Pl@ntNet; Merlin. The park layer is an application of this class, not an extension of it; the contribution here is the linkage to the witness (the living-kin sentence) and to the dedication.

**Meditation applications.** Insight Timer, Calm, Headspace, and the category's retention machinery (streaks, badges, series). The mechanism adopts the category's goal and rejects its engine; the meal-anchor plus no-streak architecture is the point of departure, and P-G3 wagers on it.

**Contemplative eating.** Plum Village's contemplative-eating practice and the Five Contemplations recited before meals ("this food is the gift of the whole universe — the earth, the sky, numerous living beings…"); the Theravāda meal-reflection (*paṭisaṅkhā yoniso…*) by which monastics have reflected on food's purpose before eating for twenty-five centuries; the Japanese *itadakimasu* ("I humbly receive"), a whole culture's one-word acknowledgment of received lives; and the grace traditions of the Abrahamic world. The mechanism claims no novelty whatsoever in the *idea* of pre-meal gratitude — the idea is among humanity's most widespread practices, which is precisely the launch thesis; the claims are confined to the witness/non-equation/dedication *architecture*.

**Location-based social mechanics.** Pokémon GO and the location-game wave (the engagement precedent and the over-gamification warning); check-in systems; the corpus's own published no-streak and pull-only discipline in the need-compiled-questlines publication.

**This corpus.** The incommensurability-preserving coupler (the parent doctrine; this mechanism is its third instance); the gift-tag-time-reveal publication (the reveal choreography family); the spent-time floor and the two-blindnesses (the doctrine that time-facts deserve honoring independent of motive — extended here to beings for whom the question of motive does not arise); and the duration-blind, moment-not-meter guard set, all previously published and CC0.

## 10 · Pre-Registered Predictions

Stated 2026-07-21, before any implementation exists:

- **P-G1 (the anti-licensing wager — the design's falsifiable core).** Users of the witness-without-discharge design will show consumption unchanged or *reduced* relative to their own baseline (meat frequency and portion patterns, self-reported and, where consented, photographically evident), in contrast to the licensing direction documented for discharge-based designs. **Falsified if** witnessed-and-answered meals show the offset signature: consumption rising as practice accumulates. The authors commit to publishing the result in either direction.
- **P-G2 (visibility does conscience's work).** Among active users, unprompted dietary drift (toward reduced or more deliberate consumption of witnessed categories) will emerge within ninety days *without any prompt, verdict, or recommendation surface existing in the application* — the mechanism contains none. Falsified if visibility without verdict produces no measurable drift.
- **P-G3 (the meal beats the streak).** Meal-anchored practice recurrence will meet or exceed category-typical meditation-app retention *without any continuity mechanic*, and — the sharper half — will show no discontinuity-collapse pattern (the streak-break cliff) in lapse-and-return curves. Falsified if removal of streaks produces the category's known retention penalty with no offsetting anchor effect.

## 11 · The Adversarial Surface

A defensive publication should attack its own mechanism. The attacks, and what answers them:

**Farming.** The dominant abuse class of reward-bearing systems has no purchase here, for the strongest possible reason: **nothing mints.** The practice produces no token, credit, score, or rank; presence-marks are scoreless; the witness log confers nothing. A farmer who automates fake meals and fake dedications harvests an empty field. This is not a mitigation but a structural absence — the abuse economy requires a reward channel, and the mechanism contains none. (The corpus's published questline mechanism makes the same move for the same reason; the two publications jointly claim the *mints-nothing* family.)

**Broadcast spam and performative flooding.** The horizontal movement is opt-in, secondary, human-verified at the provenance layer, and rationed rather than feed-optimized; with no follower counts, no virality mechanics, and no metric a flood could inflate, spam buys nothing and costs reputation among exactly the audience it hoped to reach.

**Location abuse.** Pins are coarse by default, delayable, and anonymizable; the practice site need never be the home; the child mode's no-public-pin floor is absolute (§7.3). Residual risk parallels every location-optional social system and is treated with the same seriousness: safety review before launch, conservative defaults always.

**False witness.** Malicious or erroneous meal claims (fabricated ingredients, staged dedications) corrupt only the fabricator's own private log — there being no reward, there is no fraud economy — and the public surface carries only what a verified human chose to dedicate. The graver integrity risk is the system's own recognition error, treated in §12 as a limit rather than an attack.

**Moral brigading.** The historical failure mode of visible-virtue systems is the mob that polices others' virtue. The refusal set removes its instruments: no comments on dedications, no visible consumption data of *any other user* (the witness is private by construction — one can never see what another ate, only what they dedicated), no verdicts to enforce. One cannot shame what one cannot see.

**Data poisoning of the commons.** Citizen-science contributions inherit the host platforms' identification-QA and community-verification machinery; the mechanism adds no bypass and defers entirely to those commons' governance.

## 12 · Honest Limits

**Unbuilt, n = 0.** The mechanism is design-complete and unimplemented. Every claim is architectural; every prediction is registered in advance of data; the family's own pilot history (small-n, confounded, honestly reported in prior publications) is the standard this project holds itself to, and this paper inherits it.

**The elegance caution.** The design sits at the intersection of an unusual number of converging doctrines from this corpus, and convergence density is seductive: a system this internally coherent is either deeply right or deeply self-confirming, and the two are indistinguishable from inside. The coherence earned this specification; only P-G1–P-G3 can earn the product.

**The food-guilt risk is real and is the design's gravest responsibility.** The quantified-tracking category this mechanism visually resembles has a documented pathology reaching into disordered eating. A *moral* meal-witness could inherit and amplify it in vulnerable users — guilt is a heavier payload than calories. The no-balance invariant, the absence of verdicts, and the ceremonial (never proportional) response are the structural mitigations, but structure is not sufficiency: the design requires a compassion-first UX review with eating-disorder-informed expertise before launch, an unambiguous exit from witness features for users who need one, and the explicit statement — made here — that this application is a gratitude practice, not a dietary intervention, and makes no health claims of any kind.

**Visibility is not neutrality, and the design does not pretend otherwise.** Presenting a hen's forty-two days beside her species' eight years is a bare fact with a moral gravity no disclaimer removes. The design's commitment is narrower than neutrality: *no verdict surface* — no judgment rendered, no diet prescribed, no consumption scored. On the doctrinal question itself the mechanism takes no position, and its tradition of origin gives it reason not to: Theravāda does not canonically prohibit meat-eating (the threefold-purity rule governs; the historical Buddha declined to mandate vegetarianism), and the founder's personal practice is exactly that — personal. The application shows; conscience decides.

**Recognition error contaminates the witness.** A confident misidentification is a false biography — a dedication misaddressed. Uncertainty language mitigates; it does not eliminate. The curated table's farmed durations are themselves estimates with real regional variance, and are presented as estimates. A witness must not be more precise than its knowledge.

**The instrumentation question is not dissolved.** Whether app-mediated gratitude practice deepens practice or flattens it is an open empirical and contemplative question. Phoneless bracketing is the strongest structural answer available — the device is absent from the practice itself — but bracketing is a mitigation, not a proof, and the tradition's own answer (that the practice eventually needs no instrument at all) is the horizon this product should be judged against: the best outcome for a user is to one day not need it.

**The horizontal movement can drift performative.** No-score architecture removes performance's usual rewards, not its possibility. The metric discipline (practices-inspired, never watch-time) and the opt-in secondary status of sharing are the containments; the drift is named as a permanent adversary rather than a solved problem.

**The session light is the design's most gamifiable surface, and naming it that is the mitigation's first half.** A colour that descends as one gives is one product decision away from a colour that ascends as one returns — and every constraint in §7.5 (no numeral, no total, no history, no comparison, no streak, no walk-gate) exists because the un-constrained version of that surface is the retention mechanic this whole paper refuses. The constraints are architectural, but they are also the most likely thing to be traded away under growth pressure. They are recorded here so that any future relaxation is legible as a departure rather than an evolution.

**The data commons dependency.** The park layer depends on public biodiversity infrastructure and its terms of use; the citizen-science give-forward depends on those commons remaining open. These are external dependencies honestly outside this design's control.

## 13 · Lineage and Close

*(This section carries the mechanism's cultural provenance; per this corpus's genre discipline it is labeled as lineage — grounding and translation, never mechanism. The mechanism of §§2–8 stands complete without it.)*

The dedication at this mechanism's center is not an invention. Merit dedicated to departed beings is among the oldest practices in the Theravāda world — the Tirokuḍḍa Sutta's instruction, kept alive in Cambodia as **Pchum Ben**, the fifteen-day festival in which the living dedicate merit to the departed through offerings of food. This mechanism is that grammar, inverted and made daily: the beings fed *us*; we dedicate merit to *them*. For the Khmer families this project serves first, the practice inside this application is not new technology asking for new behavior; it is the oldest behavior, given a new bowl.

The witness, likewise, has ancestors on every continent: the monastic's reflection before alms-food, the Five Contemplations of the Zen table, the Japanese *itadakimasu*, the grace said in a hundred million homes tonight. Every tradition that survived long enough to feed its children built a pause before the meal, and inside the pause, the same recognition this mechanism renders in data: *lives became this food.* The application's entire ambition is to hold that pause open for people whose traditions have thinned — and to refuse, structurally, the one modern temptation every ancestor of this practice would have recognized as corruption: the conversion of grace into account. The grace that settles nothing settles, in the end, the only thing that matters — the eater, into gratitude, before the first bite is taken and after the last.
